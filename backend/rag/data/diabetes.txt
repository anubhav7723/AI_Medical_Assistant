DIABETES

Definition:
Diabetes is a chronic condition characterized by high blood glucose levels.

Normal Ranges:
Fasting Glucose:
- Normal: 70–100 mg/dL
- Prediabetes: 100–125 mg/dL
- Diabetes: >126 mg/dL

HbA1c:
- Normal: <5.7%
- Prediabetes: 5.7–6.4%
- Diabetes: ≥6.5%

Key Parameters:
HbA1c, Fasting Glucose, BMI, Age

Additional Factors:
Gender, Hypertension, Heart Disease, Smoking History

Symptoms:
Frequent urination, excessive thirst, fatigue, blurred vision

Causes:
Insulin resistance, obesity, genetics, sedentary lifestyle

ML Feature Insights:
- High HbA1c → long-term glucose elevation
- High fasting glucose → immediate diabetes indicator
- High BMI → obesity-related diabetes risk

Complications:
Heart disease, kidney failure, nerve damage

Lifestyle & Diet:
Reduce sugar intake, exercise regularly, maintain healthy weight

User Questions:
Q: What does high HbA1c mean?
A: It indicates long-term elevated blood sugar levels.

Q: Is my glucose level dangerous?
A: Levels above 126 mg/dL may indicate diabetes.

Q: How can I reduce diabetes risk?
A: Maintain diet control, exercise, and weight management.
