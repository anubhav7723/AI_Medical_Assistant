HEART DISEASE (CARDIOVASCULAR RISK)

Definition:
Heart disease refers to conditions affecting blood vessels and heart function, often due to plaque buildup.

Normal Ranges:
Total Cholesterol: <200 mg/dL
LDL (Bad Cholesterol): <100 mg/dL
HDL (Good Cholesterol): >40 mg/dL (men), >50 mg/dL (women)
Triglycerides: <150 mg/dL

Blood Pressure:
- Normal: 120/80 mmHg
- High: ≥140/90 mmHg

Key Parameters:
Total Cholesterol, LDL, HDL, Triglycerides, Systolic BP, Diastolic BP

Additional Factors:
Glucose, BMI (via height & weight), Gender, Hypertension

Symptoms:
Chest pain, shortness of breath, fatigue

Causes:
High cholesterol, hypertension, smoking, diabetes

ML Feature Insights:
- High LDL → increased plaque formation
- Low HDL → reduced protection
- High BP → strain on heart
- High glucose → cardiovascular risk

Prevention:
Exercise, balanced diet, reduce saturated fats, avoid smoking

User Questions:
Q: Is high cholesterol dangerous?
A: Yes, it increases risk of heart disease and stroke.

Q: What is good HDL level?
A: Above 40–50 mg/dL is considered protective.

Q: How to reduce heart risk?
A: Exercise, healthy diet, control BP and cholesterol.
