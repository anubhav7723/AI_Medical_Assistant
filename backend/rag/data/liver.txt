LIVER DISEASE

Definition:
Liver disease includes conditions that damage liver function, affecting metabolism and detoxification.

Normal Ranges:
ALT: 7–56 U/L
AST: 10–40 U/L
Bilirubin: 0.1–1.2 mg/dL
Albumin: 3.5–5.0 g/dL

Key Parameters:
ALT, AST, Bilirubin, Albumin, ALP, Total Protein, AG Ratio

Additional Indicators:
BMI, CRP, Creatinine, BUN, TSH, Blood Pressure, Alcohol use

Symptoms:
Jaundice, fatigue, abdominal pain, swelling

Causes:
Alcohol abuse, hepatitis, fatty liver, obesity

ML Feature Insights:
- High ALT/AST → liver damage
- High bilirubin → impaired liver function
- Low albumin → poor liver synthesis
- High ALP → bile duct issues

Lifestyle:
Avoid alcohol, maintain healthy weight, balanced diet

User Questions:
Q: What does high ALT mean?
A: It indicates liver cell damage.

Q: Is high bilirubin serious?
A: It may cause jaundice and indicates liver dysfunction.

Q: How to improve liver health?
A: Avoid alcohol, eat healthy, and exercise regularly.
